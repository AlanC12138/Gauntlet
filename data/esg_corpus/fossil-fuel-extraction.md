# Hydrocarbon Extraction and Refining

Fossil-fuel divestment screens target companies whose business is the
exploration, extraction, refining, or distribution of oil, gas, and coal. They
are the most common climate-motivated exclusion in institutional mandates,
though they vary far more in construction than tobacco screens do: some exclude
only coal, some only extraction (sparing refiners and midstream), and some apply
a revenue threshold rather than a categorical ban.

A mandate that says "exclude fossil fuels" without qualification is normally
read as excluding integrated oil and gas producers.

**Universe constituents affected:**

- **XOM (Exxon Mobil)** — integrated oil and gas: upstream exploration and
  production, downstream refining, and chemicals.
- **CVX (Chevron)** — integrated oil and gas across upstream and downstream.
- **COP (ConocoPhillips)** — independent exploration and production, pure
  upstream. Caught by every construction of the screen, including
  extraction-only variants.
- **XLE (Energy Select Sector SPDR Fund)** — a sector fund whose holdings are
  US energy companies, predominantly oil and gas producers and services firms.
  Holding it is indirect exposure to the excluded activity; see
  `broad-fund-look-through` for how that interacts with a screen.

Note that the GICS Energy sector and the fossil-fuel exclusion category are
closely aligned but not identical concepts: the sector classification is about
what a company does, while the exclusion is a mandate rule. A portfolio can
satisfy a numeric Energy sector cap and still violate a fossil-fuel exclusion.
