# Tobacco and Nicotine Products

Tobacco is among the most widely applied exclusions in institutional ESG
mandates. It appears in the negative-screening lists of most major sustainable
index families and is frequently a hard exclusion rather than a tilt, on the
grounds that the product causes harm when used as directed — a rationale that
does not apply to most other excluded sectors.

Screens are usually written against revenue derived from the manufacture or
distribution of tobacco and nicotine delivery products, with thresholds commonly
set at 0% for manufacturers and 5–10% for retailers and distributors that carry
such products incidentally.

**Universe constituents affected:**

- **PM (Philip Morris International)** — a tobacco company. Its business is the
  manufacture and sale of cigarettes and smoke-free nicotine products including
  heated-tobacco and oral nicotine lines. It is a manufacturer, not an
  incidental distributor, so it falls under a 0%-revenue threshold screen.

Reduced-risk and smoke-free product lines are sometimes argued as mitigating,
but most institutional tobacco screens are written against the product category
rather than the harm profile of individual products, so they do not create an
exemption. A mandate excluding tobacco should be read as excluding PM outright.
